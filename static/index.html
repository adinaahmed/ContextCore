Ask AI

Multi-query search
Rephrases your question 2–3 different ways and searches for each, then merges the results. Best for: vague or multi-part questions. Trade-off: a few seconds slower.
Compress context
Trims each retrieved source down to only its most relevant sentences before answering. Best for: long, noisy documents. Trade-off: may occasionally cut something useful.

Documents

Choosing a chunking strategy
Recursive
Splits text into fixed-size pieces, breaking cleanly at paragraphs and sentences where possible. A safe default for most documents.
Semantic
Groups sentences by meaning, so each piece stays on a single topic. Better for documents with distinct sections or ideas.
Contextual
Adds short surrounding context to each piece before storing it, helping the AI understand a piece even out of order. Best for dense technical material.
